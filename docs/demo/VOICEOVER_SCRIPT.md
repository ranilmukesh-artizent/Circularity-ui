# Voiceover script: Circularity demo video

**For:** the screen recording with an AI voiceover (20–21 Oct 2026). The recording follows [`DEMO_SCRIPT.md`](DEMO_SCRIPT.md), parts 0 to 5, in about **4 minutes**. Paste each *Narration* block into the voice tool as one clip; the *On screen* line says what to record while it plays.

**Voice:** calm, plain English, about 150 words a minute. Say the numbers as written ("nine thousand six hundred dollars"). No product claims beyond what is on screen.

> Synthetic data only. Before the video is shared with any client, the narration about certification, cost sources, model learning and integration effort should be reviewed, and wording about data provenance and accountability should be reviewed by Kinective's Legal team.

| 1 | Title slide, then the User Flow slide | When a leased asset comes back, separate teams usually decide whether to sell it or scrap it, and nobody sees what it could have been worth.
