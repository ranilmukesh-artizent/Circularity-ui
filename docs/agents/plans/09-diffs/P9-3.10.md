# P9-3.10: tool-fleet numbers before and after (remaining-life model)

R8. Nothing changes until a person promotes a `rated_life` model. `model.fit {template: "tool_fleet", kind: "rated_life"}`
only stores a candidate (a Weibull per component and class, fitted on the repair log's teardown verdicts); a candidate is
never read. Promotion is the two steps of every model: a person proposes it, a different person with `approve.flows`
promotes it; an agent does neither.

## Without an active model: no change

`tool_life` rates each component as in P9-3.9 (PLM, then the pack's table `tf-life-table-1`, rules `tf-life-1`). The sample
world, RT-1048 and the industrial demo have no life inputs or no tool-fleet template: `test_po_golden.py` and
`test_screens_case.py` keep every number.

## With an active model

| What | Before promotion | After promotion |
|---|---|---|
| A component the model fitted (for example CMH Gearbox, Piston and seal kit from the 900 seeded verdicts) | `L_i` = PLM rating, else the pack's table | `L_i` = the model's median hours; source `model` |
| A component the model did not fit (under 30 verdicts, or no pack component of that name) | as before | as before |
| `life.range` of a case with telematics | none | `[RUL_tool at the P10 lives, RUL_tool at the P90 lives]` |
| `life.table_version` | `tf-life-table-1` | the model id (for example `MV-012`) |

The repair log's component names reach the pack's through `repair_aliases` (`Piston seal` to `Piston and seal kit`); a name
neither the pack nor its aliases know (for example `Motor brushes` of CMH) is fitted but rates no pack component.

Only a case valued after the promotion (a new case, or a late workbench grade that re-evaluates it) uses the model: an open
case keeps its life until then. A changed `rul` moves the valuer's thresholds as in P9-3.9, so the recommendation of such a
case can change. To list them on a stack whose sources have synced: `python -m scripts.diff_tool_fleet save <file>` before
the promotion and after it, then `report <before> <after> P9-3.10`.
