# P9-3.8: tool-fleet numbers before and after (GR-13, stale telematics)

R8. Every tool-fleet case with a remaining life (`rul`) is now also gated by GR-13 (catalogue gate `G-TOOL-TEL`, params
`max_age_days: 7`) on the routes `release` (re-issue) and `redeploy` (tool pool). When the tool's telematics were last seen
7 or more days before the clock the case is decided at, or there are none, those two routes keep their value and net but
are never the recommendation: the best of the other eligible routes is. One exception item `stale_telematics` is open for
Quality (the pack's Approver) until a fresh reading arrives, which re-evaluates the serial's open cases.

## The sample world (the prototype's 26 open returns): no change

The sample world is decided with a sample telematics reading on file (`fleet_evidence.sample_evidence`), so GR-13 passes and
the PO goldens (`equip_decisions.json`) keep their best path and net (`test_tf_02_sample_world_unchanged`).

## Cases opened from data

| Case opened from data | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| telematics seen under 7 days before the decision (`CMH-48213`, 2026-10-04: 3 days) | a recommendation | unchanged |
| telematics 7 days or more old (`CMH-48777`, 2026-09-29: 8 days) | re-issue or pool may be recommended | never re-issue or pool; the best other route; exception for Quality |
| no telematics for the serial | re-issue or pool may be recommended | the same as stale: "No telematics for this tool" |

Nets and ranks of all six routes are unchanged; only the recommendation and the exception differ. To list the changed cases
on a stack whose `fleet` and `tooltel` sources have synced: run `python -m scripts.diff_tool_fleet save <file>` on the code
before this commit and again after it, then `report <before> <after> P9-3.8`.
