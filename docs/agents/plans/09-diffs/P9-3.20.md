# P9-3.20: tool-fleet numbers before and after (fit-for-use threshold, GR-03)

R8. The threshold `rulMin` (the remaining life, in percent, a harvested part needs to be certified) is now pack data,
`customer_layer.params["valuer.rulMin"]` in `packs/tool_fleet.json`: 50, range 30 to 90, owner Quality, raise-only for
a customer overlay. The default does not change.

## At 50 (no overlay): no change

The valuer's parameters for a decision are `BASEP` with the live pack's threshold; with no overlay that is `BASEP`
itself. Over the 36 cases of the sample world (the demo's tool-fleet world, planned as one queue): **0 cases change** (route,
net of any path). `test_po_golden.py` and `test_screens_case.py` keep every number; the industrial demo, its 128 cases and
RT-1048 do not read the parameter.

## As an illustration: an approved overlay raising it to 60 (and 70)

| Threshold | Cases whose harvest value, recommended route or eligibility changes (of 36) |
|---|---|
| 50 | 0 |
| 60 | 10 |
| 70 | 16 |

Examples at 60 (net of harvest before, after; recommended route before, after): RC-26142 harvest 401 to 95, route harvest
to refurb; RC-26123 harvest 333 to 121, route harvest to resell; RC-26117 refurb, harvest 14 to -88 (the route stays);
RC-26134 route redeploy to resell. The harvest line reads "70% life ≥ 60%" for a part that still counts, and a part at 55%
drops out. These are an illustration of a customer's own tightening: no case of any org changes until an overlay is approved
by a person other than its maker, and it applies only to that org unit and the units below it.
