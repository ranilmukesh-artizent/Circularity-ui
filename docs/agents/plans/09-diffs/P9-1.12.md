# P9-1.12 before and after

Carbon factor set `cfs-2026.2` (sample, synthetic): the B 5.7 formula kg = (e_new x d - e_prep - e_log) x qty, with provenance on every factor. d is 1 on a confirmed named order (lease, so, proj), else the forecast 0.5. The device's d_rule moves from `low` to `demand`, so its channels use the forecast 0.5. Only the CO2e figures change: the best route, its net and the ranking are unchanged for every case and lot (test_co2_does_not_change_route_or_net). The industrial demo is untouched.
